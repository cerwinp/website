Who is MiSaMo?

About

MiSaMo is the first sub-unit of the South Korean girl group Twice, formed by JYP Entertainment. It consists of Twice's Japanese members: Momo, Sana and Mina. They debuted in 2023 with the extended play Masterpiece.

Members

Sana

Minatozaki Sana

Born December 29, 1996 in Tennōji-ku, Osaka, Japan, as an only child. She wanted to become a singer and dancer early on and was inspired by Girls' Generation. Besides her native Japanese, she speaks Korean.

Sana is a Japanese singer based in South Korea and a member of Twice, formed in 2015 by JYP Entertainment.

Momo

Hirai Momo

Born November 9, 1996. Known mononymously as Momo (Korean: 모모; Japanese: モモ), she is a Japanese singer, rapper and dancer based in South Korea.

She is a member of the South Korean girl group Twice under JYP Entertainment.

Mina

Myoui Mina

Born March 24, 1997. Known mononymously as Mina (Korean: 미나; Japanese: ミナ), she is a Japanese singer and dancer based in South Korea.

She is a member of the South Korean girl group Twice, formed by JYP Entertainment in 2015.
